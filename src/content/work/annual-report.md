## The Challenge

- Large volume of disconnected data and initiatives
- Limited narrative structure to guide readers
- Risk of low engagement from public audiences and stakeholders
- Needed to communicate both performance and purpose clearly

## The Approach

I shifted the report from a data-first document to a story-driven experience, emphasising the people behind the numbers.

To achieve this, I:

- Reframed content around human impact and community outcomes
- Directed photography and imagery to highlight real riders and employees
- Designed a clear narrative structure, connecting each section to a defined value statement
- Introduced headline-driven storytelling, improving scanability and comprehension
- Developed a cohesive visual system to unify diverse content across pages
- Collaborated on copywriting to align messaging with strategic goals

## The Results

- Improved readability and engagement, making complex information easier to understand and navigate
- Increased usability as a communication tool, with outreach teams actively using the report in presentations and community engagement
- Strengthened perception of organisational clarity and intentionality among stakeholders
- Created a repeatable design framework for future reports and communications
- Elevated the report from a static document to a strategic storytelling asset

## The Challenge

Public perception of Pierce Transit was limited and unclear:

- Viewed as non-essential by many residents
- Frequently confused with regional transit services
- Lacked a strong emotional or community-driven connection

The challenge was to reframe transit as a meaningful, everyday contributor to community health and opportunity.

## The Approach

Instead of focusing on service details, I reframed transit around universal human needs—themes like opportunity, local business, and staying active.

This led to a simple but scalable campaign platform:

> “Improving local transit, because [universal need] matters.”

This messaging connected Pierce Transit’s services to outcomes people already value, making the benefit immediately clear and relatable.

From there, I:

- Developed a flexible campaign system adaptable across channels
- Led copywriting and verbal identity development
- Directed visual identity, typography, and photography
- Designed and launched multi-channel assets (billboards, transit ads, bus shelters, print)
- Created a dedicated landing page to extend engagement and collect community stories
- Built a feedback loop, incorporating user-submitted stories into future campaign iterations

## The Results

- Generated 31M+ campaign impressions across out-of-home and print channels
- Drove 17,000+ landing page visits, making it one of the most-visited non-service pages during the campaign period
- Increased public engagement, with dozens of community-submitted stories
- Established a scalable campaign platform for ongoing storytelling and future iterations
- Strengthened community connection and perception of Pierce Transit as a valuable local service
- Campaign assets were used in discussions with elected officials, helping communicate impact clearly at a policy level

## The Challenge

- Severe labor shortages impacting essential transit operations
- Oversaturated hiring market with low differentiation (“Now Hiring” fatigue)
- Need to attract candidates quickly while elevating perception of transit careers

The opportunity: reposition transit roles from jobs → meaningful, fulfilling careers.

## The Approach

I identified a key differentiator: Pierce Transit offered not just employment, but stability, benefits, and quality of life.

From this insight, I developed the campaign platform:

> “Great Career. Happy You.”

This concise message reframed the value proposition around personal fulfillment rather than just employment.

To bring the idea to life, I:

- Led copywriting and campaign messaging strategy
- Directed photography featuring real employees, reinforcing authenticity and trust
- Designed a multi-channel campaign system across print, digital, fleet, and signage
- Ensured consistency and visibility across high-frequency touchpoints
- Built a flexible creative system for sustained rollout over a 12-month period

## The Results

- Increased job applications by over 50% during the 12-month campaign
- Increased hiring by ~100%, significantly improving staffing levels
- Helped Pierce Transit become one of the fastest agencies in Washington State to restore pre-COVID service and ridership levels
- Established a distinct and ownable recruitment platform in a highly competitive market
- Improved perception of transit roles as stable, long-term career opportunities

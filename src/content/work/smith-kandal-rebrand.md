## The Challenge

- Outdated visual identity that no longer reflected service quality
- Lack of clear, differentiated messaging for prospective clients
- Increasing competition from newer, more modern brands
- Needed to attract new leads without losing existing brand equity

## The Approach

I started by defining a clear brand narrative, aligning their services with the real challenges their clients face.

This strategic foundation informed all creative outputs:

- Developed core messaging and verbal identity centered on client needs and solutions
- Designed a modernized logo and visual identity, retaining key elements to preserve brand recognition
- Wrote, directed, narrated, and edited a story-driven video campaign
- Launched the campaign across regional theaters and social media, maximizing reach and emotional engagement
- Extended the identity across web, print, signage, and advertising, ensuring a cohesive brand experience

## The Results

- Increased web traffic by ~40% during the campaign period
- Generated a notable lift in new client inquiries and leads throughout the campaign run
- Expanded brand reach through multi-channel video distribution (theater + digital)
- Strengthened brand perception, better aligning visual identity with service quality
- Positioned Smith-Kandal to compete more effectively with newer market entrants

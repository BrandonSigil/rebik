## The Challenge

- Disconnected branding between church and school
- Outdated website with poor usability and navigation
- Friction in key user journeys (onboarding, finding events, accessing curriculum)
- Lack of clear ownership and structure for ongoing content management

## The Approach

I grounded the project in user research and design thinking to ensure solutions addressed real needs:

### Research & Insights

- Conducted user interviews and synthesised findings into an empathy map
- Identified pain points across what users said, did, and felt
- Translated insights into a clear problem statement to guide decisions

### UX & Website Design

- Simplified onboarding flows, reducing friction and unnecessary steps
- Defined key user journeys (events, curriculum, engagement)
- Created lo-fi wireframes to validate structure and usability early
- Developed hi-fi designs aligned with the new brand system

### Brand System

- Designed a unified visual identity connecting church and academy
- Led a multi-round stakeholder process (concepts → refinement → final system)
- Established typography, colour, and layout standards for consistency
- Built a foundation for ongoing content and brand governance

### Validation

- Conducted task-based usability tests and desirability testing
- Iterated based on both qualitative and quantitative feedback

## The Results

- Improved website usability and navigation, reducing friction in key user flows, making them 2x faster
- Streamlined onboarding experience, making it easier for new users to complete tasks
- Established a unified brand system across church and school
- Increased user satisfaction, reflected in consistently positive feedback
- Delivered a scalable framework for ongoing content management and updates
- Created a research-backed foundation for future digital improvements
